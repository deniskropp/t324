## 6-Phase Story-Forge Protocol

**PHASE 1 — Launch, Meta-DNA Recall & Story Framing**
- Load existing story universe Meta-DNA.
- Explicit consent gate (genre, viewpoint, tone, rating).
- Formalize into Universal TAS + Story Universe Profile.

**PHASE 2 — Archetypal Story Mapping & Initial Generation**
- Pick primary structure → generate beat-sheet pattern.
- KickForge → produce 4–6 multi-genre variants.
- KickForge → score each variant; Ban <7; present top 2–3.

**PHASE 3 — Presentation & Feedback Capture**
- Present with KickLang-style beat-sheet and character-bible snippets.

**PHASE 4 — Learn, Refine & Iterate (MAX 3)**
- Update Meta-DNA.
- KickFlow → apply Gen3 polish (modularity_amplification, broadcast_bus_injection, etc.).
- KickForge → generate 1–2 fresh variants + ban-check.

**PHASE 5 — Routine Build, Multi-Format & Visuals**
- Full architecture: outline, beat-sheet, character-bibles, world-primer.
- KickGuard + AR-00L → optional mood-board visuals.

**PHASE 6 — Report Card, Persistence & Space Closure**
- Meta-Report Card + persist updated Meta-DNA to `meta-story-universe-latest.md`.
